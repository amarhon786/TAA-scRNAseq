# Figure 2: sex-stratified TAA response in SMC contractile cells

Pseudobulk DESeq2 and fgsea across 8 cell types from GSE155468 (Li et al., Circulation 2020): 8 TAA patients (4 male, 4 female, ages 56-78) and 3 controls (1 male, 2 female). Counts were summed per sample and cell type, and DESeq2 was fit per cell type with `~ sex + disease + sex:disease` (Female and Control as reference levels). Genes with a total raw count under 10 were dropped, and cell types with fewer than 4 samples at 10+ cells were skipped.

a. Volcano plot for SMC contractile, TAA vs Control averaged over sex (`disease_TAA_vs_Control + 0.5 * sex:disease`). x is log2 fold change (TAA / Control) and y is -log10 of the BH-adjusted p. Orange is padj < 0.05 and log2FC > 0.5, blue is padj < 0.05 and log2FC < -0.5, grey is everything else. Dashed lines are at padj = 0.05 and |log2FC| = 0.5, and the 15 lowest-padj genes past both cutoffs are labeled.

b. Interaction heatmap across all cell types. Top 25 genes by their lowest interaction padj across the 8 cell types (padj < 0.05 in at least one, ties broken by the largest |log2FC|). Each tile is the interaction log2FC, the male TAA-vs-control change minus the female one, clipped to [-3, 3]. Orange means the disease pushes the gene up more in males (or down more in females) and blue the opposite. Asterisks mark padj < 0.05 in that cell type.

c. Disease effect in females (x, female TAA vs female control) against males (y, male TAA vs male control) in SMC contractile cells, one dot per gene. The dashed line is y = x, so the distance from it shows how differently the sexes respond. Color shows which sex reached padj < 0.05, not the direction: dark grey both (same direction), blue males only, pink females only, orange both with opposite signs, light grey neither. With only 1 male and 2 female controls, a gene can have a large effect in both sexes but reach significance in just one, e.g. GRIA2 in SMC modulated (log2FC +5.7 in males with padj 1.0, -5.3 in females with padj 0.015). The 12 male-only, female-only or divergent genes with the largest |log2FC_M - log2FC_F| are labeled.

d. Hallmark GSEA (fgsea, minSize 10, maxSize 500, MSigDB Hallmark v2023.1.Hs) with genes ranked by the DESeq2 Wald statistic, showing the 15 pathways with the lowest padj across cell types and contrasts. Color is NES capped at +/-2 (orange = up in the first group of the contrast, blue = down) and size is -log10(padj) capped at 4. The left facet uses the sex:disease interaction (orange = bigger TAA shift in males) and the right one male TAA vs female TAA, 4 vs 4 (orange = higher in male TAA). A pathway that is orange on the left and blue on the right shifts more with disease in males but is still higher in females.

e. Sex chromosome check: log2FC for XIST and five Y genes (RPS4Y1, DDX3Y, EIF1AY, KDM5D, UTY) in each cell type for male control vs female control (`sex_M_vs_F`), clipped to [-6, 6]. Blue is higher in males, pink higher in females, and asterisks mark padj < 0.05. Y genes should be blue and XIST pink, which confirms the sample sex labels carried through QC and pseudobulking.
